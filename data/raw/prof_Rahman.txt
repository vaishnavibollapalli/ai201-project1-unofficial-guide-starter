Professor: Mahfuzur Rahman
University: Georgia State University
Course: CSC 3320

Review 1:
Dude is a tough cookie. Did not like the class, but bro can teach. 
If you can sit through his lectures and briefly study each topic, you can get an A in this class. 
Grading is kinda brutal, bonus points save you and are easy to get. 
Basically, you will be humbled, but it is necessary. 
Def do C/Unix practice, it's good for your cs career. Good luck.

Review 2:
He has pretty good lectures and explains concepts thoroughly in class but is a very tough grader on exams as he is a stickler for semantics. 
Lots of extra credit opportunities (up to 7 pts) to make up for the abysmal test scores he gives out.

Review 3:
he genuinely feels like he is learning the information with you. 
His exams are ridiculously long. He does not respond to emails, but his grader tas take grading homeworks and labs very simply and were honestly the highlight of the class. 
Extra credit for tophat assignments and attendence which can boost a lot so definitely go to class.

Review 4:
I played Clash Royale every class and just studied a couple days before the exam. 
These other reviews just are not it. 
He gave bonus at the end giving like a crazy 7 point boost. I went from a 93 to a 100. 
The final is also kinda predictable. 1 question for a topic: Regex, pointers, linked list, structs, unions, for loops, etc.

Review 5:
Dr. Rahman is very pleasant, and his lectures can be a bit slow at times but he engages with students and doesn't just read from slides. 
But very paranoid about cheating and worked our class harder as the semester went on, even adding several extra quizzes and homework assignments toward the end. 
Also splits hairs over semantics on test questions.
