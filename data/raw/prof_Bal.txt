Professor: Bal Abdullah
University: Georgia State University
Course: 4520

Review 1:
Good professor, he made algorithms easy to understand and straightforward. 
His exams were based on the examples we did in class. 
Take him if you can, study for the quizzes and exams he tells you what's going to be on them.

Review 2: 
His class isn't necessarily hard, so if you study for the exams, you'll be fine. 
HOWEVER: 
- He said attendance was mandatory & passed out an attendance sheet, but it wasn't included in the syllabus' percentage breakdown. 
-His quizzes (30%) are only two questions, so if you mess up 1 question, your grade is tanked.

Review 3:
Coming from a CS student at GSU, he is one of the better ones. 
HOWEVER, you MUST attend class. If you are somebody who skips 50%+ of the time, this class WILL be hard for you. 
He has a few exams, a few quizzes, and a few homeworks. 
The workload isn't that difficult, but the material can be if you're not attending.

Review 4: 
My biggest complaint is that he is slow to grade! None of our grades are here, and it is close to the withdrawal date. 
Also one other review said this but he does not provide any study material. 
He rathers you study everything on your own and that can be hundreds of slides just to do 3 questions.

Review 5:
Bal is easily one of the best professors at GSU. 
His lectures are easy to follow, he rewards participation and he genuinely cares about his students success.
